# ITI Attendance Final V6

Corrections:
- Attendance period is strictly **10 August 2026 to 31 July 2027**.
- July 2026 is completely excluded from all month selectors and calendars.
- August 2026 is the first available month.
- July 2027 is the final available month.
- Dashboard student calendar now highlights Present dates green, Absent dates red, Leave yellow, and Holidays blue.
- Dates outside the attendance period are disabled.
- Calendar alignment remains Sunday through Saturday.
- Working-day calculation remains automatic from the calendar: Sundays and manually marked holidays are excluded; Saturdays are not automatically holidays.
